# travel-planner
A Travel Itinerary Website offers a comprehensive suite of features designed to simplify and enhance the travel planning experience. Users can create custom itineraries tailored to their preferences, budget, and schedule, with options for multi-city trips and destination suggestions. The platform allows users to book activities and experiences, such as guided tours, adventure sports, and cultural events, as well as accommodations ranging from hotels and resorts to unique stays like treehouses or villas. Transportation services, including flight bookings, car rentals, and public transport options, ensure seamless travel logistics. The website also provides travel guides and tips, offering insights into must-visit places, local cuisine, and cultural norms, along with tools for budget management, such as cost estimators and expense trackers.
